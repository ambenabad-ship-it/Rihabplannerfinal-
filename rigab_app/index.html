Review & confirm deliveries
One screen per sales rep — call each rep once, handle all their clients together

The review is grouped by sales rep so you call each rep only once. Every screen shows the rep's phone number and all of their clients stacked below, each with their orders and buttons to Confirm, Cancel, or Replan individually (or in bulk per-client). Canceled and replanned orders free their stock instantly — previously out-of-stock clients may reappear under the same rep or another. Everything auto-saves, so reloading won't lose your work.

No default ship: nothing is delivered unless you explicitly click Confirm. Pending orders are not shipped. A live "Rescued (earlier reps)" counter warns if a cancel or replan under the current rep freed stock for a client you already passed.
